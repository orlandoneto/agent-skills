# Journalist pitch compressor

## Goal

Compress long PR copy into a 3-paragraph newsroom pitch.

## Inputs

Long brief / press release

## Instructions

1. Keep news hook in paragraph 1
2. Proof + data in paragraph 2
3. Offer interview/assets in paragraph 3
4. Add subject line

## Output

Compressed pitch
